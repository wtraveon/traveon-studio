Frequently asked questions

Good questions before a great website.

How long does a website project take?

A standard starter website typically takes 2–4 weeks after the required content, feedback and access are received. More involved projects receive a tailored timeline.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review the current site, identify the most important improvements and propose a scope for the redesign.

Can you add online booking or e-commerce?

Yes. Booking systems, storefront connections and other third-party integrations can be included when the platform and requirements are confirmed. Subscription and platform fees may be separate.

Do you work with businesses outside North Carolina?

Yes. Traveon Studio is based in Wilson, North Carolina and works with local and remote clients.

What do I need before we start?

Helpful starting materials include your business details, services, preferred contact information, brand assets and any existing website or social links. We can identify remaining content needs during discovery.
