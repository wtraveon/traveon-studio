FAQ

Questions before you start?

How much does a website cost?

The Website Starter is $1,800. Advanced e-commerce, booking, custom databases, content migration, or specialized functionality is quoted separately.

What is included in the Website Starter?

Up to five pages, custom design, mobile-responsive development, a contact or inquiry form, basic on-page SEO, approved business integrations, two revision rounds, and 30 days of post-launch support.

How long does a website take?

Most standard small-business websites take approximately 2–4 weeks after all required content, access, and project information are received.

How does payment work?

$300 starts the Website Starter. The remaining $300 is due after final approval and before launch, domain transfer, or final file delivery.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included for reasonable changes within the approved scope. New pages, major redesigns, or new integrations are quoted separately.

Do you work outside Wilson, NC?

Yes. Traveon Studio serves Wilson and surrounding areas and also accepts remote clients.
