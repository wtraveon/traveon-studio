Privacy

Privacy at Traveon Studio

Consultation information stays focused on evaluating and responding to your project inquiry.

Consultation data

What may be collected

Your name, business name, contact details, business type, website or social link, project interest, and the project summary you choose to provide.

How it is used

Consultation information is used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate project fit, discuss scope, and maintain reasonable business records.

What not to submit

Do not submit payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through the consultation form.

Abuse prevention

The consultation form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to verify submissions and applies request throttling. The application stores a one-way digest of the requester IP address for rate limiting rather than the raw address; older rate-limit entries are purged during subsequent form requests. Cloudflare may also process technical browser and request information to provide verification and protect the form.

Form delivery

Consultation submissions are processed through Traveon Studio’s server-side form endpoint, validated on the server and stored as business inquiry records so the studio can respond and maintain reasonable project records. Hosting, email delivery, domains, booking, store, or other approved integrations may involve third-party providers with their own privacy practices.
